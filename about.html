The Mission

The primary purpose behind the creation of Qadri AI is to serve humanity by providing a dual-purpose intelligence engine. It is meticulously engineered to assist users in their Dunyawi (worldly) tasks—such as coding, cybersecurity, and automation—while simultaneously offering authentic Deeni (religious) knowledge based on the teachings of the Ahl-e-Sunnat wal Jamaat.
